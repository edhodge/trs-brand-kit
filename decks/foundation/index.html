Logo

Logo rules.

Rectangle · 2:1
Square · 1:1
Transparent
Master logo: white wordmark with a horizontal red bar beneath. Designed to sit on the brand charcoal (#3E3E3E) or any dark surface. A light-surface (ink-on-white) version is not supplied; don’t recolour the master to fake one.
Files supplied (SVG, vector): assets/trs-logo.svg (transparent) · assets/trs-logo-rect.svg (rectangle, 2:1) · assets/trs-logo-square.svg (square, 1:1).
Don’tRotate or skew the wordmark.
Don’tInvert to ink-on-white. No light-surface master exists.
Don’tPlace on any colour other than charcoal or ink.
Don’tStretch, squash, or recolour the red bar.

07 / 15 — Casing

Spacing

4px base. 50px section anchor.

Every gap, padding and offset is a multiple of 4. Below: the scale, with the real surface each step belongs to.

4
Hairline gaps. Star-rating dot to text. Stock dot to "In stock" label.
8
Tight pairs. Variant pill internal padding. Grid gutter between product cards.
12
Inline spacing. Icon to label inside CTAs. "Free shipping" eyebrow lockup.
16
Component padding. Inside pricing-pack cards. Between FAQ rows. Header nav-link gap.
24
Stack rhythm. H1 to lede. Lede to first paragraph. Above the Add-to-cart row on PDP.
32
Group separation. Between buy-box and trust list. Above star rating on review cards.
40
Sub-section break. Between pricing packs and variant picker. Pack row top margin.
50
Section anchor. Top & bottom padding on every full-width section (hero, pricing, FAQ, footer).
64
Hero stack. Between hero copy and the buy panel on desktop PDP.
120
Page-level break. Major editorial gap, e.g. between "Designed by Robert Rock" and the trust strip.

Page width: 1340px desktop · 360px mobile design unit.
